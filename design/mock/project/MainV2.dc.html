Questions

Where does the data come from?

Each store's own public pages: policies, shipping, checkout and homepage. Every fact links to the quote it came from.

How fresh is it?

Promotions, products and content are checked weekly; everything else on the 1st of each month. Each fact shows the date it was read.

Is it allowed?

We read only public pages, obey robots.txt and skip stores whose terms forbid it.

Is my store listed? Can I correct it?

Search for it above. Each store page has an "Is this your store?" link.
